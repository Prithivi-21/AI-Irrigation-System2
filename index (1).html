AI-Based Smart Irrigation Management System

A decision-support system that reads soil moisture, temperature and rain forecast, then tells the farmer whether to switch the water pump on or leave it off.

Water conservation

Water is used only when the soil needs it, cutting waste.

Smart farming

Data-driven decisions replace guesswork in daily farm care.
